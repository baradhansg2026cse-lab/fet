Paris, the capital of France, is celebrated for its elegant architecture, rich history, and lively cultural scene.
The city is home to iconic landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower, Notre-Dame Cathedral, and the Louvre Museum, while its streets are filled with cafés, bookstores, and galleries.
Set along the River Seine, Paris blends grand boulevards with intimate neighborhoods, making it a place where art, fashion, food, and everyday life come together beautifully.
